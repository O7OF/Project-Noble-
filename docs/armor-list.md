## Rules

- **Haunted**: needs every other helmet and attachment bought
- **EVA [C]**: needs EVA bought
- **[R] chests**: need the base chest bought
- **UA/CHOBHAM, UA/NxRA, Tactical/Trauma Kit**: need Tactical/Hard Case bought
- **mcc_only**: added by MCC seasons, not in the 2010 Armory; MCC Series 8 helmets (Belos, Blackguard, Drengr) omitted
- Items with no cR price (pre-order, Limited/Legendary edition and Waypoint rewards: Recon UA/HUL[3], Officer Elite, Legendary and Eternal effects, Military Police, CQB, Multi-Threat [W], Base Security [W]) need another unlock in Noble, e.g. a commendation or challenge milestone.
- Armour colours: 30 (10 bases x 3 shades): Steel/Silver/White, Red/Mauve/Salmon, Orange/Coral/Peach, Gold/Yellow/Pale, Sage/Green/Olive, Teal/Aqua/Cyan, Blue/Cobalt/Sapphire, Violet/Orchid/Lavender, Crimson/Rubine/Pink, Brown/Tan/Khaki.
