A Unity shader for "H3" (Discord link in L281) was said to work for most Reach assets; Reach shader for Unity attributed to PointDevice or FiyCsf (chiefster_4014, 2024-06, L280-281).
- Reach Elite recolouring is an open, unanswered question: the "Elite IK chief elites v1.4" pack, and the user wants MP-Spartan-style recolour; also asked for the hex of the glowing bits, guessing the material is base_illum_cards (x_crimsongalax, 2025-07, L643-644). - Reach Spartan Blender pack quirks: clicking the visor shader on a CQC/UA/HUL helmet turns the helmet white and the visor is not selectable alone (x_crimsongalax, 2025-07 and 2026-02, L623-624, L885-886, unanswered). In Soul's Reach SFM models, changing colour recolours armour, techsuit and lights together (gyro.stormbles, 2025-10, L725, unanswered).
- Reach Spartans.zip is by swagster (Drive link in L696); channel link for the Reach Blender pack used by gameoverload to say Reach is easier to recolour than Infinite (gameoverload, 2026-01, L838, also L299). visor_cc is only the CC mask for every visor (confirmed by raw L697).
- Reach-pack baking: bake via the armor shader by tabbing in and routing the nodes that feed the BSDF into Material Output as emissive; the result came out darker than the live view (thefinalfronti, muchacho5894, 2025-11, L762-766). Later swagster asked for the green channel to be flipped; helmet and visor still looked wrong in places (muchacho5894, 2025-12, L782). A Reach MK VI helmet bake fault (killerboy3699, 2025-02, L502) and a baking-tool conflict between two Reach helmets with identical nodes (ifhq, 2024-07, L287) were also unanswered.
- Dark or wrong-looking Reach/H3 items reported with no fix: Reach Halsey textures very dark (jacobmt05, 2024-11, L426); helmet coloured but body "all dark" (cinnamint626, 2024-10, L385); black patches on ripped H3/ODST with copied pack shaders (cythecy_, 2024-07, L296).
- Tint multiplies the base: a hair tint did nothing because the base colour is natively black (gameoverload, 2025-10, L721). General principle usable for a dark piece.

## Best leads for these problems
1. Black or too-dark Reach Elite piece (Honor Guard shoulder). Slice has no tag-level answer, only analogies: (a) a tint multiplies the base, so a black or near-black base or albedo stays black (L721); (b) mask polarity white = colour applied (L851), so an inverted or empty CC alpha or mask leaves the piece untinted or dark; (c) H4 pattern, control map alpha = CC zones with white = primary, grey = secondary (chiefster, 2025-01, L466), a useful hint to check whether the Reach equivalent has the piece in a grey/black zone; (d) data textures must be Non-Color (L268); (e) a green-channel flip for Reach normals (L667, L782); (f) check UV overlap or tile when textures bake black (L749); (g) a missing texture or alpha input drives opaque black (L249). Fiy's open Reach shader (gitlab FiyCsf/open-halo-reach-spartan-shader-for-unity) is commented and could show how Reach CC, spec and masks are combined (L72). Nothing here covers fresnel tint or spec mask specifically.
2. Elite shine/spec wrong. Leads: IOR 1.45 and BSDF replacement for old shaders (L358); SSS on metal (L335); CE cubemap reflection chain (L784-787, L857-859); official mod tools hold shader code to check specular coefficient and tints (L601); Reach Elite recolour is unsolved in this channel (L643).
3. Importing custom armour to Reach. Reach .jms cannot be extracted because the import pipeline changed; use Foundry Blender addon for tags and Reclaimer for compiled maps (L401); use original H3EK .ass source rather than Reclaimer geometry (L814); match bone names and scale (L93); Reach Sapien supports player simulation (L312); asks for Reach vehicle, sbsp and CE-to-Reach imports are unanswered (L309, L599, L809, L871). No answer on armour permutations or Foundation (L100).
4. Networking of custom armour for other players. Nothing relevant: only unanswered anti-cheat or EAC-off questions (L112, L125, L341) and a Reach MP overlay idea. Those suggest custom content only reaches others in custom games hosted with mods and anti-cheat off; this is inference, not stated.
