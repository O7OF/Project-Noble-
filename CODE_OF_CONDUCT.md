# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Project Noble to be a welcoming place for everyone who wants to help,
regardless of experience, identity, or background. We pledge to make
participation a harassment-free experience for all.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique the code, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Ask questions before assigning blame.
- Keep discussion on topic and in the right channel (issues for bugs,
  discussions for design, pull requests for code).
- Respect the game's ownership rules: never ask for or share MCC files, keys,
  or extracted assets.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spamming, derailing, or repeatedly reopening closed decisions.
- Sharing copyrighted game data or tools that bypass ownership checks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues/PRs, or temporarily or
permanently ban contributors who violate this code. Report problems privately
to the maintainers (see SECURITY.md). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
