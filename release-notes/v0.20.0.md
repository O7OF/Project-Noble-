# Project Noble Release Notes --- v0.20.0

This release brings major engine enhancements, complete Elite armor customization, custom visor colors in-match and over network, Forge AI spawn fixes, and UI/menu overhauls.

### Elite Customization & Armor Pieces
- **All Elite Sets & Pieces:** Full support for Honor Guard, Counselor, H2A Ranger, H2A Armor, and Schism sets and pieces in the Armory and in-match.
- **Elite Armor Abilities:** Equipped Armor Abilities (Jet Pack, Evade, Drop Shield, Armor Lock, Active Camo, Hologram, Sprint) now visibly mount and display correctly on Elites.
- **Multiplayer Customization Replication:** Player character models, species, and selected armor permutations sync accurately across multiplayer clients.

### Custom Visor Colors
- **In-Match & Networked Visors:** Extended and preset visor finishes (including custom RGB visors) render accurately in-match on both Spartans and Elites and replicate over the network.

### Forge Spawnable AI & Netcode
- **Authoritative Forge AI Spawning:** Fixed the issue where non-host clients spawned duplicate or static invincible AI clones. Spawning is now authoritatively coordinated with the host.
- **Netcode & Sync Stability:** Hardened session synchronization and packet delivery across all game modes.

### UI & Quality of Life
- **Loading Screen Emblems:** Restored the classic NOBLE and UNSC emblems and animated diamond grid background cleanly with zero text overlap.
- **Pause Menu Overhaul:** Redesigned in-game pause menu with tactical framing, smooth scrollwheel navigation, and Service Record-styled Challenges panel.
- **Camera & UI Stability:** Smooth local 3rd-person and vehicle cameras, and stable focus transitions upon match completion.
