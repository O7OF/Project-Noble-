# v0.19.59

## Campaign launch declares exactly the players it is for

The player count in the launch block is once again `1 + guests` with nothing added. Declaring more players than are
actually present leaves the engine waiting at load state 8 for participants that never arrive - which is what made
every campaign launch hang in v0.19.56 - and the pre-game lobby already passes its whole roster, so a three-player
lobby declares three.

Also noted for the record: appearance is not an issue - each player's armour reaches the others in co-op, confirmed in
a live two- and three-player session.

Three-player campaign co-op still ends with the co-op error after a short while. That is not a player-count cap (a
three-player lobby already declared three before this build): the engine ends the mission through the campaign
compatibility path, the same one that boots a second player until it is suppressed. The fix there is to make the
joining player's campaign record match the host's, which is the next piece of work.
