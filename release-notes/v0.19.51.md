# v0.19.51

## Lobby roster: a player who leaves is removed, and no longer shows up as "Host"

When a guest left the pre-game lobby their row stayed behind with no name, and the roster drawing labels a nameless
row as "Host" - so the host saw a ghost player called Host sitting in their lobby.

The roster is now sanitised whenever it is adopted from the master server: nameless rows that are not the host are
dropped, exactly one row is treated as the host, and that row is given a name. The dev build also reports its version
again (it was still saying 0.19.49).

Known still open: a guest joining a campaign co-op mission spawns without the host's checkpoint, falls and dies.
That needs the insertion point to follow the host rather than the guest's own (empty) campaign progress.
