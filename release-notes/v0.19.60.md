# v0.19.60

## Join handshake: guests time their start on the host

A joining client no longer guesses when the host's match is ready. While its match is running, the host broadcasts a
`session_sync` control frame over the relay every two seconds with its frame number and running state; a guest -
including one joining in progress - waits for it before posting its campaign start command instead of using a fixed
2.5 second delay.

`BlamEngine` logs `host reports its simulation running (frame N); starting` on the guest when the handshake lands, or
`no session_sync from the host after 20 s; starting anyway` when it does not (LAN joins without the relay fall back to
the old timing).

Known open: a guest that joins a match already in progress can be seated in the session while their client still draws
the pre-game lobby. That is on the client side of the join (the launch trigger or its relaunch), and the guest's log
will show whether `Guest launching from lobby` was reached.
