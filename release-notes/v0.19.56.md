# v0.19.56

## Campaign co-op declares its four-player capacity, so join-in-progress fits

Three players ended with the co-op error after a short while. The launch log shows why: the launch block's player
count (`+0xE8`) is set to the roster present *at launch*, so a two-player session declares two players. When the third
player joins in progress they are over the session size, and the engine ends the mission instead of seating them
(the same path that clears its game-running flag).

Campaign co-op now declares four players - the mode's capacity - regardless of how many are present at launch. The
wait-for-peers barrier is unchanged, so a host still does not wait for players that are not coming.

Added after this build: v0.19.55's boot-reason 0x1B suppression and the frame-numbered game-running trace.
