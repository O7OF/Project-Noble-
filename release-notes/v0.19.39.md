# Project Noble v0.19.39 Release Notes

## Scoreboard & Multi-Team Enhancements
- **Dynamic Match-Accurate Scoreboard.** Headers, colours, totals, and footer score pills now dynamically adapt to the teams actually active in the match. Single-team or non-standard team matches no longer default to a split Red vs. Blue header with empty columns.
- **Local Player Team Priority.** `SetScoreboardRoster` now prioritizes the team passed at launch (`-lobby-teams`) over cached session roster polls, preventing initial side desyncs where a player's nameplate could briefly show an incorrect team header.
- **Reliable Team Dispatch.** The launcher now passes `-lobby-teams` whenever any player has an assigned side, preventing variant cache lag from withholding team definitions.
- **Yellow Team Alignment.** Team 6 in the lobby and scoreboard palette is now Yellow, matching Halo: Reach's internal engine order from Forge spawn points (Neutral, Red, Blue, Green, Orange, Purple, Yellow, Brown, Pink).

## Forge Mode & In-Match Team Switching
- **Forge Red/Blue Team Pinning.** The engine restricts Forge sessions to Red and Blue sides; the Forge lobby now reflects this restriction by offering Red/Blue selection only. Switching the lobby mode to Forge automatically re-deals any 8-team roster across Red and Blue, while Custom Games retain the full 8-team picker.
- **Authentic In-Match Team Switching.** Changing teams mid-match now triggers an instant respawn (`object_kill` on the engine thread via `DevCheats`), respawning the player on their new team with updated armor colors immediately, matching authentic Halo: Reach behavior.

## Camera System
- **Vehicle & Player Cameras.** Integrated third-person and cockpit camera handling for vehicles and players.

## Crash Diagnostics & Logging
- **Universal Minidump Generation.** Minidumps (`noble-crash.dmp` for Release, `noble-dev-crash.dmp` for Dev) are now generated adjacent to the executable for both SEH crash filters and `std::terminate` unhandled C++ exception handlers.
- **Dedicated Dev Logging.** Dev builds now write to `noble-dev.log` while Release builds write to `noble-client.log`, always located beside the executable.
- **Diagnostic Hooks.** Added `-crash-test` to test and verify exception and minidump capture, and migrated the `-team-probe` research hook onto the engine thread to access thread-local game state datums safely.

## Version
- Bumped to **0.19.39** (`CMakeLists.txt`, `Version.hpp`, `BuildIdentity.hpp`, `noble.rc`).
