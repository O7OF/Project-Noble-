# Project Noble v0.20.36

## Forge World Updates
- **Forge Menu Names:** The Pelican, Spectre, Boathog and orbital beacons now have names in the Forge menu (for example Needler Spectre, Gauss Boathog, MAC Round and Spirit Drop: Wraith) instead of blank rows. Update Noble Forge World in MODS > RECOMMENDED MAPS to get them.
- **Check for Updates:** MODS > RECOMMENDED MAPS now checks whether your Noble Forge World is the newest one. It shows "Up to date", or UPDATE AVAILABLE with an UPDATE button that downloads the new map. CHECK FOR UPDATES checks again.
- **Fixed:** DOWNLOAD in v0.20.35 could fetch an older Forge World pack without AI paths. It now always gets the newest pack.
- An older Forge World no longer shows as Installed, so MAP AI and PLACE AI don't fail on a map without AI paths. If PLACE AI says the map has no AI paths, it now tells you where to update.
