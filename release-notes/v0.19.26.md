# Project Noble v0.19.26 Release Notes

## Network Replication of All Elite Armors, Halo 2 Pieces, 30 Spartan Visors & In-Game Elite Armor Effects

### 1. In-Game Elite Armor Effects
- **pmcg List Redirection (RVA `0x10580C`):** Verified and maintained the patch pointing the Elite armor effects lookup from the empty `pmcg` list at `+0x8C` to the fully-populated effect list at `+0x80`.
- **Elite Effect Flag Force (RVA `0x105837`):** In retail `haloreach.dll`, `0x105837` loaded `r8b` from `dil` (`mov r8b, dil: 44 8A C7`). In matches for Elites, `dil` is computed from `record[0]` bit 1 (which Bungie set for Elites to suppress armor effects). When `apply_selection` (`0x105BDC`) was called, it tested `r8b` (`bpl`) and skipped applying the effect tag if `bpl == 0`. Patched RVA `0x105837` to `mov r8b, 1` (`41 B0 01`), guaranteeing that all seven armor effects (Flames, Blue Flames, Heart Attack, Pestilence, Inclement Weather, Birthday Party) attach and render in-game on Elites as well as Spartans.
- **Appearance Record Bit-1 Guard (`ElitePack.cpp`):** Cleared bit 1 of `copy[0]` in `BuilderHook` to ensure `dil` is always 1 during unit composition.

### 2. Network Replication of All Elite Armors & Halo 2 Pieces
- **Network Appearance Updater Call Redirection (`ElitePack.cpp`):** Added RVA `0x2C64DF` (the session appearance processor in `0x2C63D8`) to `kBuilderCalls` (`{0x04AC36, 0x0447B0, 0x2C64DF, 0x3B184F, 0x524CD5}`). Previously, remote unit appearance updates in multiplayer called the unhooked `0x1055EC`, completely skipping custom piece unpacking and marker checks.
- **Robust Local Player Datum Identification (`RecordSetHook`):** Replaced fragile command-line `-connect` heuristic with direct inspection of `_player_is_local_machine_bit` (`player_flags & 0x08`) and `player[0x2DE] == 1` in `IsLocalPlayerDatum`. Players joining via the in-game Server Browser (without CLI `-connect`) now reliably identify their local player datum and write their custom Elite sets (including H2A Ranger, Counselor, Honor Guard, and mixed custom pieces) into the settings synchronization block (`0x2C837C`).
- **Full Preset Preservation:** Maintained all 11 rows in `kSetPieces` (including H2A Ranger preset at index 9 and Counselor preset at index 10). Remote peers reading the sync block now faithfully unpack marked pieces and display the exact custom armor.

### 3. Full Spartan Visor Network Replication (All 30 Finishes & Custom RGB)
- **Profile Visor Conversion Patch (RVA `0x2EDC5E`):** Replaced `call 0x3B2F4; mov edx, eax` with `lea edx, [rcx - 0x9D]; nop` (`8D 91 63 FF FF FF 90`). Profile item IDs 157..186 now translate directly to internal visor indices 0..29 without stock 0..4 clamping, allowing `player_set_visor` (`0x2C7F10`) to accept and store all 30 visor indices into `[player + 0x98]`.
- **Unlock Check Bypass for Visor & Armor (RVA `0x2C7DCC`):** In retail `haloreach.dll`, the appearance packet serializer (`0x2C7A8C`) checked `0x2C7DCC(player->index, 2, visor_index)`. If `0x2C7DCC` returned 0, the engine wiped the visor byte in the network packet back to 0 (Default). Patched `0x2C7DCC` to `mov eax, 1; ret; nop` (`B8 01 00 00 00 C3 90`), unlocking all items and preserving the chosen visor index across the network.
- **Extended Visor Sync Block (`ElitePack.cpp`):** Spartan sync blocks now carry the 30-visor index in `block[15]` and custom linear visor RGB in `block[28..31]` with marker `0xFE`. `BuilderHook` ensures remote Spartan bipeds apply `record[15]` directly to `[player + 0x98]`.
