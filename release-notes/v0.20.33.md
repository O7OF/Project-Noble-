# Project Noble v0.20.33

## Host Tools
- **Campaign Host Menu:** Hosts get new rows in the pause menu: restart the mission, go to the next mission, revert to the last checkpoint, pause the game for everyone, and return to the lobby with your players or on your own.
- **Kick and Ban Players:** Hosts can kick a player (they can't rejoin for 2 minutes) or ban them for 30 minutes, 1 hour, 24 hours or for good. Bans follow the player's account, so a new name doesn't get around them. The lobby shows your ban list, and you can lift a ban.
- **Mute for Everyone and Co-hosts:** Hosts can mute a player's voice and text chat for the whole game, and make a player a co-host who can kick and mute too.

## Lobby
- **Lock Your Lobby or Add a Password:** New JOINS option: open, invite only, password or locked. Players already in your lobby always stay, also after a match, and friends you invite can still get in. Password lobbies ask for the password in the server browser.
- **Anti-AFK:** Hosts can remove players who stop playing after 3, 5 or 10 minutes. Players get a 30 second warning, and cutscenes and loading screens don't count.

## Chat
- **Mute Someone's Chat for Yourself:** Every player can now hide one player's chat messages on their own screen, from the lobby or the scoreboard.
- **Word Filter:** Hosts can hide slurs in chat, plus their own list of words. It is off by default; the first-time setup asks if you want it.

## Server Browser
- **Clean Server Names:** Server names no longer show slurs or swear words, also when spelled with numbers, symbols or other spellings. Only whole words are hidden, so normal names like Assistance or Class stay the same.

## Career
- **No Credits for Very Short Games:** Leaving or restarting a game in under 5 minutes no longer pays credits, challenges or commendations. Games that end normally pay as before.

## Campaign
- **Restarts Wait for Everyone:** When the host restarts a campaign mission or Firefight game, the lobby now waits for every player to come back before starting again.

## Fix
- **Disbanding a LAN Lobby:** Players in LAN and direct games now leave properly when the host disbands the lobby.

## Dedicated Server
- **Kick and Ban Commands:** The server console now has working players, kick, ban, bans and unban commands.
