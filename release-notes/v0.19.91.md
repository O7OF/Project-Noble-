# Project Noble Release Notes --- v0.19.91

## Theater Mode Saved Film Playback & Controls Fixes
- **Continuous Film Playback Beyond 15 Seconds:**
  - Resolved premature playback aborts / kicks to main menu after ~15 seconds / 900 frames ("An error occurred during film playback" / `Slot 4 reason=7` / `Slot 6 GameResults`).
  - `NetworkTransport` initialization is bypassed for local Theater playback, eliminating false peer-timeout disconnections and watchdog aborts.
  - Installed detours on `c_game_engine::abort_game` (`0x3ABB70`), `c_saved_film_manager::session_monitor` (`0x3ABE70`), and `c_game_engine::set_game_ended` (`0x3C08B4`), keeping saved film errors cleared and allowing films to play continuously to match completion.
  - Suppressed game-over screen triggers, post-game carnage awards, and match completion packets during saved film playback.
- **Keyboard & Mouse Pause Menu Support in Theater:**
  - Fixed keyboard `Escape` not opening the pause menu in Theater mode due to `uiPointer` detection locking out hotkey polling.
  - Added alternative hotkey support (`F10`, `Pause/Break`) alongside `Escape` to toggle the pause menu.
  - Added an interactive on-screen `[ESC] MENU` button in the upper corner during Theater playback so mouse users can click directly to open the pause menu.
  - Menu options in Theater dynamically adapt to display "LEAVE THEATER" ("Stop watching the film and return to the Theater menu.") and cleanly return to the menu interface.
