# Project Noble 0.9.16.24

A standalone Halo: Reach client for Halo: The Master Chief Collection on Steam. This build focuses on a stable menu, the Armory camera, and in-app updates.

## Highlights

- **In-app auto-updater.** The main menu checks `O7OF/Project-Noble-` at launch. When a newer release exists, an **UPDATE AVAILABLE** panel shows the version and download size; the client downloads it, then **RESTART & INSTALL** swaps the files and relaunches. Settings, appearance and mods in `data\userdata` are preserved.
- **Main menu stays on the title scene.** The menu no longer switches from the cliff camera to the campaign camera a few seconds after startup. No more sliced / zoomed-out background.
- **Armory framing fixed.** The Spartan and Elite are framed head-to-toe, large enough to fill the stage, with the pedestal weapon hidden below the frame. No 2.3x-4.1x 2D pixel upscale.
- **Menu supersampling.** Only the menu process renders at 1.5x its window (capped at 3200x1800), so the enlarged Armory view stays sharp. Match processes render at native resolution, so gameplay performance is unchanged.
- **Windowed and borderless are deterministic.** Windowed is a fixed-size, movable window that always fits the monitor work area. Borderless full screen creates a monitor-sized window. Display changes are saved and applied on restart.
- **Nameplate cleanup.** Retired community/event plates are removed from the Armory. Developer and insider plates are available in `noble-dev` only and are hidden and unequippable in release builds. Saved plates keep their original catalog positions.

## Install

1. Download `Project-Noble-v0.9.16.24-win64.zip` from the assets below.
2. Extract the whole folder somewhere writable (for example `Documents\Project Noble`).
3. Run `Noble.exe` (or `play.bat`).

Requirements: Windows 10/11 x64, Steam with **Halo: The Master Chief Collection** and the **Halo: Reach DLC** installed, and a Direct3D 11 GPU.

## Notes

- The client verifies Steam ownership at launch.
- Your settings and appearance live in `data\userdata` and are never overwritten by an update.
- The release zip intentionally excludes the development `data\mods` folder; put your own mods there.
- The menu map is included so fresh installs work without copying files from another installation.
