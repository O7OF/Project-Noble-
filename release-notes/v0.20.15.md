# Project Noble Release Notes --- v0.20.15

### Extended Party Cards, Nameplate Fixes & Online Login Flow
- **[UI] Party Widget & Lobby Player Card Overhaul:** Redesigned the top-right party card and lobby player rows into unified tactical cards, integrating rank badges, emblem frames, and status indicators with zero alignment jitter.
- **[UI] Full Nameplate Artwork Rendering:** Eliminated edge cropping on player nameplates, ensuring all custom banners, emblems, and insignia (such as Insider badges) render completely without cutoff.
- **[ACCOUNT] First-Launch Login Flow:** Streamlined startup onboarding so selecting Regular/Returning Player or New Player immediately opens the login menu to sign in or register.
- **[ONLINE] Server Browser & Online Play Gating:** Gated Server Browser and online multiplayer behind active logins, automatically defaulting sessions to local LAN play mode when signed out.
