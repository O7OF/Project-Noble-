# Project Noble Release Notes --- v0.20.9

### 3rd-Person Camera & Dynamic Reticle
- **Engine-Level 3rd-Person Hooks:** Integrated 3rd-person camera support directly into the Blam! engine camera pipeline with smooth field-of-view handling and customizable offsets.
- **Dynamic Crosshair & Reticle Projection:** Implemented dynamic screen-space reticle projection based on simulation aiming raycasts and CHUD anchor calculations, ensuring crosshairs accurately reflect the player's true point of aim in third person.
- **Local Vehicle Cameras:** Hardened per-client vehicle first-person and third-person camera separation for jitter-free viewing while seated.

### Scoreboard in Every Mode
- The scoreboard now seeds its roster from the lobby roster that launched the match (`-loading-roster`), so Campaign, Firefight, Custom Games, Forge, LAN and direct-connect matches show real player names and mark your own row cleanly.
- Team columns stay mode-correct: Campaign and Firefight use the unified co-op column, team columns appear only when the match has multiple teams, and Forge has no scoring rows.
