# v0.19.58

## Campaign missions load again (solo and with mods)

v0.19.56 declared a four-player session for every campaign launch. A solo launch then sat at 1% with the engine in
launch state 8, waiting for players who were never coming - so stock missions and campaign mods alike hung on the
loading screen.

The four-player capacity is now declared only for a host that is actually expecting guests; a solo launch declares
one player again.

Verified: stock m10 and the Improved Trooper Campaign m10 both reach launch state 11 and render in-mission.
