# v0.19.63

## Campaign mods built on Firefight / Multiplayer canvases

* **Campaign mods on non-campaign canvases now load and play in Campaign mode.** Workshop campaign mods built on Firefight or Multiplayer map canvases (such as **The Test** / *The Swarm* on `ff_unearthed`) now have both their `scnr` mission `map_id` (`+0x10`) and `campaign_id` (`+0x0C` `0 -> 1`) patched at load time when mounted into the `m10` campaign carrier slot, passing `main_game_load_map` validation without aborting back to the menu.
* **Campaign carrier & manifest routing.** Campaign launches always preserve the `m10` carrier and `gameModeTag = 1`, direct CLI launches of custom campaign scenarios (`-map <scenario> -mode campaign`) automatically route through `m10`, and mods with MCC manifest descriptors (`CampaignInfo.json` / `ModInfo.json`) only appear in the game modes their manifests declare.
