# Project Noble v0.19.30 Release Notes

## Native Engine Spawning, Dev Cheats & Freecam Fixes

- **Player Detection & Game-Thread Engine State:**
  - Migrated local player unit resolution from the non-functional `s_getLocalUnit(0)` to the verified Blam! engine `players` pool (`root + 0x18`, 0x490-byte records, unit handle at `+0x28`) with `PickReference` biped scan fallback.
  - Fixes early return bug that previously prevented Godmode, Super Jump, Super Speed, Noclip, and ProcessActions from executing on the game thread.

- **Item Give & Spawning Pipeline:**
  - Standardized all item and vehicle spawning on Tyler's verified `object_placement_data_new` (RVA `0x49CC24`) and `object_new` (RVA `0x46D6E0`).
  - Added fallback tag resolution: checks donor cache, active object pool (weapons, vehicles, equipment, bipeds), and loaded scenario `tagTable` (`0xC1A600`).
  - Implemented immediate inventory equipping (`s_unitAddWeapon` at RVA `0x48F778`) paired with hands-level placement (`pos + [0, 0, 0.4]`) for instant weapon give.

- **Native Blam! AI & Squad Spawners Integrated into Dev Menu:**
  - Added direct buttons for Tyler's tested engine scripts:
    - **Spawn AI Horde (F6):** Places AI squads via `s_aiPlace` (`0x6C2B10`) and moves actors to the player.
    - **Place All Squads (F7):** Places every scenario squad defined in the map.
    - **Spartan Crowd (F8):** Toggles a formation of 128 Spartan bodies in front of the player.
    - **Stress Test (F9):** Batches 500 copies of map weapons/objects.

- **Freecam "Legs Walking Away" Fixed:**
  - Standardized Freecam controls to standard PC flight: W/A/S/D to fly, Space to ascend, Ctrl/C to descend, Shift to boost.
  - Freezes player character velocity and strips WASD movement inputs from the engine input buffer (`st[0x004 + vk]`) while Freecam is active, preventing the player Spartan from walking away into the distance.

- **Godmode, Super Jump & Super Speed Verified:**
  - Godmode: Blocks damage (`0x1ED320`) and death (`0x47CAA8`) while continuously restoring shields (`0x49DD58`) and writing 100% health directly to biped health/shield offsets.
  - Super Jump: Boosts upward velocity (`vel[2] = 0.12f * multiplier`).
  - Super Speed: Multiplies horizontal velocity (`vel[0]`, `vel[1]`) dynamically.
