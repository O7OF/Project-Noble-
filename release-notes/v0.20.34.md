# Project Noble v0.20.34

## Firefight
- **Extra Enemies:** New host option in the Firefight lobby: add 2, 4, 6 or 8 extra Covenant squads to every wave. A wave only ends when they are dead too. On Courtyard the first wave goes from 28 enemies to 36 with +4 squads and 48 with +8.
- **Wave Pacing:** Hosts can make the breaks between waves, rounds and sets shorter (half as long) or longer (one and a half or two times as long).
- **Wave Squads, Dropships and Bonus Round in Game Options:** GAME OPTIONS for Firefight now has a Firefight Waves group: enemy squads in the opening, main and boss waves and in the bonus round, dropships on or off, and the bonus round length.
- **Allied Elites:** Optional host setting: up to 5 squads of Elites fight on your side, follow the players and come back after they have all died.

## Host Tools
- **Votes:** Players can call a vote to kick a player, end the round or end the game. Hosts choose which votes are allowed and can turn voting off.
- **Next Map Vote:** After a game, players can vote on the next map from the lobby.
- **Team Controls:** Hosts can balance or shuffle the teams, and lock them so players can't switch.
- **Restart With Everyone:** Hosts can restart a multiplayer game and bring every player back with them.
- **Friendly Fire, Betrayal Booting and Team Changing:** These social settings are now in GAME OPTIONS for custom games.
- **Disband From the Match:** The host's in-match menu now has DISBAND LOBBY.

## Lobby
- **Join in Progress:** Hosts can stop players from joining a game that is already running.
- **Lobby Rules:** Hosts can write a short set of rules that every player sees at the top of the lobby.
- **Reorder Playlists:** Playlist entries can be moved up and down with LB / RB or Ctrl + Up / Down.
