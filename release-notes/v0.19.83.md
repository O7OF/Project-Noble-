# Project Noble Release Notes — v0.19.83

### Campaign Rendering & Merge

- **Campaign no longer stays black after the intro cutscene:**
  - Every Extended engine-limits profile patches the engine renderer. On campaign maps those patches left the frame black after the opening cinematic (reproduced on m10 with Extended (V16), Extended + Particles and Full), even though the mission and local player unit were loaded.
  - Campaign launches now use the stock engine DLL. The saved ENGINE LIMITS setting is unchanged and still applies to Forge, multiplayer and Firefight.
  - Verified on m10: the cutscene renders and the mission hands over to normal gameplay (played through for 185 s with the player spawned in). The same check was run across the base campaign missions.

- **Cutscene skip recovery:** the `cinematic_skip_stop_internal` detour is installed again, but only its safe fade clear is kept. The 0.19.82 HaloScript command batch and cinematic/camera TLS resets that broke mission intros remain disabled (opt-in with `NOBLE_CUTSCENE_RECOVERY=1`).

### Merge

- Merged Tyler's Trooper-mode removal and the cutscene-recovery opt-in fix with the BlamEngine/ReachHost work. The two conflicted files (`src/engine/ReachHost.cpp`, `src/hooks/EnginePatches.cpp`) keep both sides: the engine-native cinematic skip request and host slot 9, and the opt-in recovery gating.
