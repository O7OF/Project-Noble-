# Project Noble v0.20.21 Release Notes

## Overview
Project Noble v0.20.21 delivers critical fixes for in-game audio initialization and Campaign Join-In-Progress (JIP). Game audio, music, and sound effects now initialize and play immediately on match start across all game modes (Campaign, Firefight, Forge, and Multiplayer) without requiring player death and respawn. Clients joining Campaign and Firefight sessions in progress no longer get stuck on a black screen or loading lock, seamlessly transitioning directly into active gameplay.

## Highlights
- **Match-Start Audio Synchronization Across All Modes**: Live mixer volumes and the engine audio thread work queue are now synchronized immediately upon DLL mapping and during the opening frames of gameplay. Resolved the bug where in-game audio would fail to initialize or play correctly in Campaign until a player died and respawned.
- **Campaign Join-In-Progress Black Screen Resolution**: Fixed an issue where non-host clients joining Campaign or Firefight matches in progress spawned their biped into the world but remained stuck on an opaque black rasterizer screen fade. Joining clients now automatically clear obsolete scenario screen fade overlays and synchronize directly with the host's active game session.
- **JIP Loading State & Auto-Skip Polish**: Corrected guest session start detection in ReachHost so joining players transition out of the loading screen immediately once their biped spawns or the map state is active, and automatically skips opening cutscenes for late joiners to attach viewport and controls directly to live match simulation.
