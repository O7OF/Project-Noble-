# Project Noble 0.19.22 (Closed Source)

## What's New in v0.19.22

### Out-of-the-Box Character Customization & Audio Bundling
- **Bundled Armor Permutations & Elite Models (`data\armor`):** The release distribution package now directly bundles `data\armor` (including `rr_elite_pack.map` and associated metadata: `.rrreloc`, `.rrneeds`, `.rrpmcg`, `.rremotes`). Players now have full Spartan and Elite armor customization available out of the box without missing permutation assets.
- **Bundled Audio Decoder (`tools\vgmstream`):** The release package now bundles `vgmstream-cli.exe` and its audio decoding libraries in `tools\vgmstream`, ensuring full custom menu music, sound effects, and voice playback out of the box without requiring manual dependency installs.
- **Packaging Pipeline Hardening:** Updated `tools/publish-release.ps1` to automatically verify, stage, and copy both `data\armor` and `tools\vgmstream` from root or release folders directly into the standalone distribution zip, while strictly maintaining clean separation from userdata, save files, and developer binaries.

## Install

Use the in-game **UPDATE AVAILABLE** banner on the main menu to update automatically, or unzip `Project-Noble-v0.19.22-win64.zip` over your existing installation. Your settings, career progress, and custom appearances are saved locally in `data\userdata` and are never overwritten.
