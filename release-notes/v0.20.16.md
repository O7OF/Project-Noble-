# Project Noble Release Notes --- v0.20.16

### In-Match Multi-Team Selection & Seamless Team Switching
- **[GAMEPLAY] In-Match Multi-Team Tactical Selector:** Replaced the binary team toggle in the Pause Menu with a full tactical team selector modal supporting all 8 multiplayer teams with live color accents and squad badges.
- **[ENGINE] Native Team Synchronization & Instant Respawn:** Integrated instant engine team synchronization and clean biped respawn cycles when changing teams mid-match, updating spawn points, armor team colors, scoreboard rows, and voice chat channels.
- **[FORGE] Seamless Forge Team Switching:** Enabled instant team switching across all 8 teams in Forge without respawn interruption or simulation pause.
- **[CONTROLS] Tactical Team Menu Controller & Keyboard Parity:** Full D-Pad, thumbstick, face button, arrow key, and mouse navigation for selecting squads in-game.
