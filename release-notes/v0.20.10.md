# Project Noble Release Notes --- v0.20.10

### Post-Match Keyboard & Mouse Focus Stabilization
- **Intentional Mouse Motion Deadzone:** Implemented unified deadzone filtering for physical mouse movement across all menu and post-game screens (`NobleUi::IsMouseMoved`). Stationary high-DPI mouse sensors no longer produce sub-pixel jitter that steals focus or fights keyboard/gamepad navigation.
- **Eliminated Focus Fighting:** Keyboard arrow navigation and D-pad selections no longer revert or flicker when the physical mouse is resting over a different button or list row.
- **Hover Sound De-duplication:** Suppressed repeated hover sound triggers when the pointer remains over the same button or during focus swaps.

### Post-Match Input Settling & Click Leak Prevention
- **Transition Settle Guards:** Enforced transition settle windows when entering the post-match results panel and when returning to the launcher Main Menu / Lobby, preventing in-game shooting clicks or edge inputs from leaking through.
- **Mouse Release Protection:** Mouse clicks are strictly ignored until all physical mouse buttons have been released following match conclusion, ensuring gameplay fire inputs never click through onto menu items.
- **Accidental Button Skip Protection:** Clicking during the stats reveal sequence now safely skips the reveal animation without triggering the underlying QUIT or RETURN buttons underneath the cursor. Buttons are armed with a clean post-reveal grace timer.

### High-Capacity Session Slot Management
- **32-Player Peer Seat Math:** Integrated upstream peer seat structure math optimizations and bounds alignment in `SlotMemberCaves.inc`, improving session stability for expanded 32-player multiplayer lobbies.
