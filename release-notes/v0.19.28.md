# Project Noble v0.19.28 Release Notes

### Developer Mod / Cheat Menu (`+` Key)
- **Universal Gamemode Availability:** Added a dedicated Developer Mod & Cheat Menu bound to the `+` key (`VK_OEM_PLUS` and `VK_ADD`). Accessible across Campaign, Firefight, Forge, Custom Games, and Multiplayer.
- **Godmode & Invulnerability:** Hooks `object_cause_damage` (RVA `0x1ED320`) and `object_kill` (RVA `0x47CAA8`) to block all incoming damage and death events for the local player biped and vehicle, while continuously restoring shields via `0x49DD58`.
- **Super Jump & Super Speed:** Simulation tick integration with configurable sliders for jump multiplier (up to 10x) and locomotion speed (up to 8x).
- **Noclip / Ghost Flight:** True free-flight noclip bypassing collision physics with full 6-DOF controls (WASD, Space = Ascend, Ctrl/C = Descend, Shift = Boost) and adjustable flight velocity.
- **Freecam / Detached Spectator Camera:** Seamless camera detachment via `Hook_FirstPersonUpdate` and `Hook_CameraFollow` / `Hook_CameraFollowingUpdate`, allowing unrestricted spectator camera panning (Arrow keys, E/Q, Shift = Boost).
- **Infinite Ammo & Bottomless Clip:** Carried weapon magazine and reserve ammo locks (`+0x3030` and `+0x3034`), alongside an instant Refill Ammo & Shields action.
- **Dynamic Item & Vehicle Spawner:** Integrated spawner catalog supporting all UNSC weapons, Covenant weapons, Armor Abilities, and Vehicles with search filtering and one-click spawning directly in front of the player.
